Up to 24 characters.

Upper and lower case matter. Only a hash goes to the relays, not the phrase.

🔒 Note: Direct Peer-to-Peer connection. Your IP address is visible to your peer. Use a VPN if you wish to remain fully anonymous.

  • The room ID is the first 32 hex characters of the SHA-256 of the lowercased phrase.
  • The connection is WebRTC, without TURN. On a very restrictive network the other person may not show up.
  • Nothing remains after you leave or close the tab.
